How the views are made

φ is read from each trained field’s checkpoint (training seed 87358) and sampled at heading 0 on a grid of 0.25 cells up to 28 × 28 and 0.5 cells above (warpspaces.py). Stretches are finite differences of that grid, relative to ntrl-demo’s map, whose input moves 1/E per cell (E = n + 0.1). Walls are mapped through the same grid. The heading has its own factor, listed per warp; none of these warps bends it by position. Success is the share of MPPI runs that reach the goal without touching a wall (planning seeds 0/1/2, pooled over the training seeds listed).
