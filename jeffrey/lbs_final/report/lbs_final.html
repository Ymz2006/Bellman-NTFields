LBS final results: LBS against ntrl-demo, RRT-Connect and LazyPRM

Queries solved, average over the sizes that are complete. Maze, 2-D: ntrl-demo 53.3 % (3 of 4 sizes), LBS 97.8 % (3 of 4 sizes), RRT-Connect 69.7 %, LazyPRM 90.5 %; Gap maze, 2-D (10 × 10 base): RRT-Connect 92.9 % (3 of 4 sizes), LazyPRM 99.8 % (3 of 4 sizes).

66 of 136 scene × planner results are in. Cells marked (k/3) pool the instances finished so far.

What was run

Scenes
Maze 2-D: 10, 14, 18, 22 cells a side, three mazes each (maze seeds 1000 i + n). Gap maze 2-D: the 10 × 10 maze cut into four blocks pushed apart by an open band of 6 k cells, k = 1, 2, 4, 8 (16, 22, 34, 58 cells a side), three base mazes each. The SE(2) rectangle robot. Gibson 3-D: a point robot flying in a 2 m slab of the main floor of ten houses: the five of the earlier 3-D test and the five houses with the smallest main floors (Collierville, Benevolence, Onaga, Coffeen, Forkland).
Queries
500 random (start, goal) pairs per scene: starts uniform over free space at least 0.1 cells from obstacles, goals drawn from the scene’s 48 random goal poses, every pair certified solvable by its ground-truth path. Gap mazes keep their goal mix (half of the goals inside the maze blocks, half in the open band). A table cell pools the three instances: 1,500 queries.
Planners
ntrl-demo and LBS: ntrl-demo’s MPPI on the trained travel-time field (training seed 87358, epoch 5,000), planning seed 0, step budget 3 × the true path. RRT-Connect: OMPL 2.0.1, default range. LazyPRM: a Python port of OMPL’s LazyPRM (k = 5 neighbours, lazy vertex and edge checks, a fresh roadmap per query). Both sampling planners get 10 s per query, then OMPL’s path simplifier.
Success
The path reaches the goal and every configuration on it, at 0.005 cells per coordinate, is collision-free under the exact geometry. The sampling planners check motions at that same resolution, so their paths never fail the scorer.
Time and length
Wall time to the finished path (MPPI until it reaches the goal; sampling planners solve plus simplification), on two CPU threads, measured while the GPUs trained other runs. Path length in cells: x-y length for the 2-D robot, x-y-z length in the houses (one cell is 0.25 m). Mean ± standard deviation over each planner’s solved queries.
