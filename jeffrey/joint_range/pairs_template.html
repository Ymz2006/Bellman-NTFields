Cabinet training pairs

ntrl-demo trains on pairs of arm poses (x0, x1). Each pose is drawn where its gripper ends up, and a thin link joins the two grippers of a pair. x0 always has its gripper in the test area and lies close to an obstacle; x1 is a random joint-space step from it. In-cabinet pairs are Test 3's new data: at least one of the two grippers is inside the cupboard. Mixture shows what each Test 3 run trained on, a share of in-cabinet pairs with in-area pairs for the rest, next to that run's planning result. In-area pairs keep both grippers in the test area. The old recipe is the registered baseline's data, where x1 can go anywhere. The page holds a fixed random subset of each set; the slider shows the first N.
