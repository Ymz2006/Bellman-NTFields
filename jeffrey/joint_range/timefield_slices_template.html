Cabinet time field

Drag the red gripper handle to place the arm. The two slices show how long the arm takes to bring its gripper from there to every other point of the test area, cut through the gripper: on the left the trained ntrl-demo network's prediction, on the right the ground truth from the clearance speed. Each gripper position uses one arm pose found by inverse kinematics on a 2.5 cm grid. Switch to Speed to compare the network's speed with the speed it was trained to match. Hover a slice point to see the true fastest route and the arm pose there; click it to move the start.
