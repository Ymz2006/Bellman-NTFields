Test 4 time field

The fields trained in Test 4, against the ground truth of their cabinet. Original cabinet: 2 cm panels. Strong cabinet: 10 cm panels, each compartment the same size inside. In each, one network was trained with one third collision samples (the arm touching or inside the cupboard or table, labelled with the lowest speed) and one on mixed pairs only. Pick a gripper position with the red handle, the sliders or a click on a chart: the charts follow the gripper along x, y and z and show the travel time from there (top) and the speed (bottom). Full arm draws the arm's collision spheres, the shape the clearance is measured on.
