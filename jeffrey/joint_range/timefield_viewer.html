Cabinet time field

Pick a gripper position: drag the red handle, use the x, y, z sliders, or click a chart. The three charts follow the gripper along x, y and z through that point. Each shows, at every position on its line, the travel time from the picked point (top) and the speed (bottom): the trained ntrl-demo network against the ground truth from the arm's clearance. Every position uses one arm pose from inverse kinematics.
