Re-creating 0.55 m · ±0.46 m · 0.60 m high

The placement 0.55 m · ±0.46 m · 0.60 m high, three times: the original runs on the rented machine, the same pipeline retrained on this workstation (RTX 3090) on the original training data and test queries, and the whole pipeline re-created here from scratch, which builds new test queries (88 of 300 are the same as the original ones, because the GPU sampler is not bit-reproducible across cards). Each set of ten configurations is picked by the same rule from its own runs. The arms are drawn as the collision spheres the checker tests. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to move. The numbers are in jeffrey/arm_placement/README.md (last section) and repro/compare.md.
