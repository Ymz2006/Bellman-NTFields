Follow-up: rows built on the integral of 1 / speed

You then asked for rows built on travel time, the integral of 1 / speed along the line, in place of speed itself. The sin/cos fit now targets 1 / speed, and each row is the running integral of that fit from the point where the line enters the maze. The integral of each sin/cos term is again a sin/cos term, and the constant becomes a ramp, so the row is still a sum of sin and cos. A 30-term row matches the exact integral with R² {{p2r2_int22}} on 22×22.

I ran the three versions you described: the summed integral as 16 rows, the per-term integrals as separate channels, and integral rows next to speed rows. The integral removes the damage the speed rows did, and adds a little over empty rows, but it does not beat the octaves.
