Leak: share of wall segments and goals with no rise on the high side. Rise: median height of the ridge in cells (truth about 9.4 on 22×22). Jump: T difference between the two sides, divided by the true difference. Sep: T divided by corridor distance for wall-adjacent cell centres at least 4 corridor steps apart. Bumps: local minima of T per true path. Frozen: the study's rows of both endpoints pinned to their median. Means over seeds.
