What this suggests

Embed travel distance, not speed. The distance rows use the exact maze graph, so they are a mechanism control and not a method. A practical version would have to learn something like them, for example from early estimates of T, and that is untested.

They also did not help everywhere. On 13×13 they reach {{geo13}} %, against {{zero13}} % for empty rows and {{octave13}} % for octaves. The jump there is already above 1 in every arm, so there was no missing separation to add. Together with the 22×22 result, that fits the idea that these rows help only where separation is short.
