Follow-up 2: the integral embedding alone, without octaves

You pointed out that the integral rows above only sat on top of the octaves, and that your idea was to drop the octaves and use this embedding alone. I ran that. The network's 256 inputs become 240 integral rows, one per line direction, plus 8 sin/cos rows of the heading alone, so position reaches the network only through the integrals.

Alone, the integral embedding plans at the level of ntrl-demo's random rows, about 40 points below the octaves. The exact integral reaches {{p3_exact13}} % on 13×13 and {{p3_exact22}} % on 22×22. On the same training seed ntrl-demo reaches {{p3_ntrl13}} % and {{p3_ntrl22}} %, and the octaves {{p3_oct13}} % and {{p3_oct22}} %. The per-term matrix and the integral combined with speed rows fail outright.
