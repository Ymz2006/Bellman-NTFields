Cabinet training poses

Each point is one anchor pose from the frozen cabinet training bank, drawn where its gripper (grasp frame) ends up. The bank holds collision-checked anchors with transitions each. The same anchors are the pool that the segment-DP pairs and split points come from. This page uses a fixed random subset of ; the slider shows the first N of it.
