The scene

A 0.30 × 0.13 rectangle car parks nose-in in bay A and has to reach bay B: back out (−x), move down (−y), drive in (+x). The lot is the unit square, about 3.3 car lengths a side, closed by outer walls. The divider between the two bays is the wall that gets thinner. Every query is a parked pose in one bay to a parked pose in the other (50 A→B, 50 B→A), certified by a collision-free witness path.
